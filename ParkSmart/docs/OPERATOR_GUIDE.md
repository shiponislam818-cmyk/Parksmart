# ParkSmart — Gate Operator Quick Guide

## Start of shift
1. Open ParkSmart and sign in with your email (or phone) and password.
2. The **Gate counter** opens. Check the facility name at the top; change it with the **Facility** box if needed (the choice is remembered on this PC).
3. The bar at the bottom shows **Live** (green dot) and the free/occupied count per vehicle type. If it shows **Offline**, check the network — your typing is kept and you can press the button again when it reconnects.

## Vehicle entry (left panel)
1. Type the **number plate** (e.g. `Sylhet Metro-Ga 11-2345`). Spaces, dashes and capitals do not matter.
2. Click the **vehicle type**: Motorcycle, Car / CNG or Microbus / SUV.
3. The **suggested slot** is the nearest free slot. To use another one, click **Change** and pick a free slot; **Auto** goes back to the suggestion.
4. Click **Register entry & print QR ticket**. The ticket appears — click **Print ticket** or let the driver photograph the QR, then **Done**.

Messages you may see:
- *"Plate already parked at … slot …"* — the vehicle never exited. Process its exit (or lost ticket) first.
- *"Facility full for Car / CNG"* — no free slot of that type; send the driver to another facility.
- If the driver has a reservation, the reserved slot is assigned automatically.

## Vehicle exit (right panel)
1. Click **Start camera** and show the QR ticket, **or** scan it with the USB scanner into the ticket-code box, **or** type the code, then **Find**.
2. Check the plate, slot, entry/exit times, duration and **Fee to pay**.
3. Take payment:
   - **Cash paid** — records cash, closes the visit, frees the slot.
   - **Online (sandbox)** — opens the test gateway. If the payment fails the vehicle stays parked; retry or take cash.
   - Monthly-pass vehicles show **Close (monthly pass, BDT 0)**.
4. A green message shows the receipt number — click **View / print receipt** to print it.

## Lost ticket
1. In the exit panel click the **Lost ticket** tab.
2. Type the plate and click **Find parked vehicle**.
3. The lost-ticket penalty is added to the fee. Take payment as normal.

## Other tasks
- **Live slot map**: click any slot to see who is parked. A free slot can be marked **Out of Service** (a reason is required) and later **Restored**.
- **Parking sessions**: see who is parked now, search by plate, and reprint a ticket.
- Only an Administrator can change a fee. Ask them if a driver disputes the amount.
